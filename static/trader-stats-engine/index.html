Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Download the file, then in Google Sheets choose File, Import, Upload. You may see a yellow "Allow access" bar. It only controls whether screenshot thumbnails load in the Gallery tab.

Does it work in Excel?

Yes, it opens in Excel as it is. The screenshot gallery thumbnails work best in Google Sheets.

Can I track more than one account?

Yes. Add each prop firm or live account as a row on the Accounts tab and pick which one you are viewing.

Can I change the checklist rules?

Yes. Each rule is a header cell you can rename. Leave a header blank to switch that rule off.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a journaling and analysis tool. It does not predict the market or tell you what to trade, and trading carries risk of loss.
