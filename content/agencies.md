## Who this is for

- Marketing, creative or content agencies selling SEO, social, content or paid work on retainer
- Agencies with 5 to 30 staff, plus founders and small teams (solo founders included) who want the same systems for their own business
- Your team already uses AI tools, but it isn't built into how you deliver
- You want more margin or more capacity, not another tool subscription

**Probably not a fit:** anyone looking for a one-off $50 workflow.

## What I build

<div class="tech-card">

### Owner Pipeline System

A voice-trained LinkedIn engine for the agency owner. It researches topics your buyers care about, drafts in your voice, and waits for your approval before anything goes live. It is the same system that posts for me Monday to Saturday.

**Best first project** if new business depends on you finding time to post.

</div>

<div class="tech-card">

### Delivery Automation Sprint

One system that removes a recurring job from your team, built in two to three weeks. Most agencies start with one of these:

- **Client reporting:** pull the numbers, write the summary, send the report, every month, without someone building it by hand
- **Content production pipeline:** brief to draft to approval to scheduled post, with your team approving instead of copying and pasting
- **Review management for local clients:** collect feedback, score it, draft replies and alert the right branch manager when something is wrong

</div>

<div class="tech-card">

### Support Retainer

Monitoring, error alerts, fixes, and one improvement every month. Systems drift when tools change. This keeps them working.

</div>

## How I handle your clients' data

- I build inside **your** accounts wherever possible. Your credentials stay with you.
- AI provider data sharing stays switched off. Client work never runs on shared or training-enabled projects.
- Every system ships with error alerts and a backup, and a written handover so your team knows how it works.
- You own everything I build.
